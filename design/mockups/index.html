Design A - Paper, Android and iOS

Chosen direction. Implementation follows the iOS frames on both platforms; the Android page is kept as an earlier exploration. Open a direction's pages for full-size frames. Sources: AUDIT.md, PLATFORM-RESEARCH.md.

Audit - top issues in the current build

  • Display hierarchy inverted: 56 px bold expression in accent over a smaller answer in a second accent.
  • No type system: default Roboto, proportional digits, ad-hoc sizes; About references an unbundled font.
  • Settings look like a debug panel: a tinted slab, stacked controls, a 17-color swatch wall.
  • Muddy light-mode keys, and a red AC block that outweighs equals.
  • Two different converter layouts, a hard-coded blue logo, hyphen used as minus.

Platform support in Flutter (checked 18 Oct 2026)

  • partialMaterial 3 Expressive: not in the SDK. Official work started in material_ui (the M3E IconButton has landed, other components are pending). The mocks build M3E on stock M3 theming plus custom widgets.
  • not yetLiquid Glass: not in Cupertino. cupertino_ui glass PRs opened on 17 Oct and are unmerged. The mocks use a Cupertino base with blur, tint and rim glass (Flutter BackdropFilter) on floating chrome only.
  • On iOS: SF Pro for chrome and the brand numeral font for keys. CupertinoIcons in the app.
