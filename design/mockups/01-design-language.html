Design language: Material 3 Expressive

Google's current design system, applied to every screen of the app, and tuned so one design language holds on both Android and iOS. This page is the token and component reference. Page 2 is the screens, page 3 is the app icon and assets.

M3 Expressive - I/O 2026, current Google recommendation 10-step corner radius scale 30 type roles (15 baseline, 15 emphasized) spring motion tokens, not duration curves dynamic color + 3 contrast levels one system, Android + iOS

1. Direction

Four decisions that shape everything else. The app is a utility: it should feel calm and fast, with expressiveness spent deliberately on the few moments that matter (a calculation completing, a rate refreshing, a unit being chosen).

Expressiveness, spent where it lands

Big type for the two things that matterresult + expression
Motion physics on every tapspring, 120-280ms
Shape morph on pressfull to 12dp radius
Color for state, never for decoration aloneroles, not hex

One system, two platforms

Scroll physics on iOSbouncing
Sheets, dialogs, menus on iOSsame layout, iOS insets
Safe areas, notch, home indicatorboth platforms
Why this route and not stock widgets. Material 3 Expressive is not in Flutter's SDK, and the Flutter team has said it will not implement it. Flutter has moved Material into an official package (material_ui, needs Flutter 3.47+, which this app already runs), and that package is where Expressive support lands incrementally. The plan is to build on that package plus a thin token layer of our own, so the app is never stuck waiting on the SDK. The exact trade-off is on page 2.

2. Color

Sixteen semantic roles, generated from one seed so light and dark stay in step, and so Android's Material You can swap the seed for the user's wallpaper color on request. Nothing in the app should ever hard-code a hex value again.

The seed (your brand color)

Your app already has a seed: Color(0x207CDF), a blue, used by both theme/light.dart and theme/dark.dart. Keep it, or switch to one of these. Either way every role below is generated from one seed, so this stays a one-line change forever after.

207CDF your current blue · 6750A4 violet · 00696D teal · 4A6363 slate · 8B5000 amber · 984061 rose. The rest of this page uses the violet M3 baseline so the token values are stable regardless of your choice.

Three overrides to delete, not keep. Both of your current themes hand-override roles that M3 generates correctly, and in dark mode that is what breaks the app: secondary: Colors.white.withOpacity(0.04) makes every field and keypad bed nearly invisible on a black surface, and errorContainer: Colors.black on a black surface is zero contrast. The light theme's secondary: white 0.5 is low contrast too. The redesign drops all three and lets the scheme carry them.

3. Typography

Fifteen roles in a baseline weight and an emphasized weight. Emphasis is the Expressive signature: the same role, one step heavier, used to make the thing you should look at louder without making it bigger. The app needs five of these roles in earnest and the rest only for edge cases.

10. What needs your decision

  1. Seed color. Violet, indigo, teal, slate, amber or rose, or Material You only (seed follows the wallpaper). Recommendation: violet as the app default with Material You as an opt-in, because a fixed seed keeps the app recognisable when Material You is off.
  2. The app's typeface. Google Sans Flex (Android-native feel), Roboto Flex, or the platform default. Recommendation: Google Sans Flex with Roboto Flex fallback, both variable, so the ROND axis is available for Expressive letterforms.
  3. Component build route. Two options, detailed on page 2: build the Expressive layer ourselves on Flutter's official material_ui package, or adopt the community material_3_expressive package (44 ready widgets, but an unverified single publisher and it requires replacing Material imports across the app).
  4. Contrast levels in Settings. Ship all three, or standard only. Recommendation: ship all three, it is a system expectation on Android 13+.
